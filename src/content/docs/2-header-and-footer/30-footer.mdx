## Layouts

- **Newsletter, navigation and wordmark** — a signup band with your brand line, menu columns underneath, and a sliver of a large wordmark at the bottom edge.
- **Minimal, centered** — your mark, one row of links, the copyright line and social media icons.
- **Brand and four navigation columns** — a classic footer with your logo and text, up to four menu columns, and a bottom bar with payment icons and selectors.
- **Fit line** — the fit line as the footer: a large signup line, a *Shop by fit* ruler, your menu columns, the bottom bar and the wordmark across the full width of the page.

## The Fit line layout

In the **Fit line** layout, the four fit zones run along a ruler as large links — *Close*, *Regular*, *Relaxed* and *Oversized*, or the names you set in **Theme settings > Fit line > Zone names**. Each link opens the **Fit collection** filtered to that zone. Leave **Fit collection** empty to link to all products.

The zone filter comes from **Fit filter** in **Theme settings > Shapes and sizes**. Without it, each link opens the collection unfiltered. See [Fit filter](/docs/getting-started/theme-settings#fit-filter).

## Wordmark

Turn on **Show large wordmark** to set your store name in large type across the footer. **Wordmark text** changes what it says — for example a shorter brand name. **Logo image** and **Logo width** set the smaller mark in the brand area; without an image, the store name is shown.

## Footer menus

Add a **Footer menu** block for each column and choose a menu. Menus with more than seven links are shown as a compact inline index so the footer stays short. Use a **Links** block for a hand-picked column without creating a menu, or a **Text** block for an address or opening hours.

## Signup form

The footer signup adds subscribers to your Shopify customer list with email marketing consent. If a page already has a **Newsletter** section, the footer form hides itself on that page so shoppers never see two signup forms at once.

## Colorway rule

Choose a **Colorway rule collection** to draw a thin band above the footer, with one segment per product of the collection (up to 16) in that product's color — its `custom.color` metafield, else its first color swatch, else the color scheme's **Fit marker** color. Leave it empty to hide the rule.

## Follow on Shop

**Show Follow on Shop** adds Shopify's Follow button, so customers can follow your store in the Shop app. It appears when Shop Pay is active on your store.
