## Hero

The hero is the most flexible first section. Choose a **Layout**:

- **Split — text beside image** with up to two stats and a floating product card. Set **Width** to **Edge to edge, 50 / 50** for a full-width band, and choose which side the image sits on with **Image position**.
- **Full-bleed — text over image** with an optional background video.
- **Full-bleed — text in a panel** for busy images where text needs a calm background. The panel uses its own **Panel color scheme**, placed by **Content position**.
- **Statement — type only** for a bold line of copy.

The floating card in the split layout shows a **Floating card product** — its title, `custom.subtitle` metafield and link fill in when you leave the card fields empty.

Set a separate **Mobile image** for the full-bleed layouts when your desktop image is very wide. For background video, upload an MP4 in **Content > Files** and keep it short — it plays muted on a loop, and shoppers can pause it with the pause button.

When the hero is the first section on a page, its heading becomes the page's main heading and its image loads first.

## Slideshow

Several slides, each with its own image, copy and button. The image sits beside a copy panel with a slide counter, previous and next arrows, and one bar per slide. Choose the side with **Image position**.

With **Autoplay** on, the active bar fills over the **Time per slide** and then moves on. Autoplay always includes a pause button, stops while the pointer or keyboard focus is inside the slideshow, and never runs for shoppers who prefer reduced motion.

Pick a **Product** on a slide to draw that slide's bar in the product's colorway — one band per color option. The product also fills in the slide's image and button link when you leave those empty.

## Image banner

A single wide image with a caption — a quiet break between sections. Choose its **Shape**. **Full width** runs the image edge to edge; turn it off to keep it inside the page width.

Add **Eyebrow**, **Heading**, **Text** and **Button** blocks to show a solid text panel docked to a corner of the image. Choose the corner with **Text panel position** and its colors with **Text panel color scheme**. On phones the panel sits under the image. Without blocks, the section shows the image and its caption only.

## Page header

A title band for pages, collections and blogs, with optional breadcrumbs, an image and a product or article count. Every part falls back to the page being viewed, so an empty section still shows the right title.

Choose a **Style**:

- **Plain** — a large title with the intro, date or count beside it on one line, closed by a rule. This style has no image.
- **Editorial** — title and intro, small print at the foot and an image column on the right.

**Show date** shows when a page was published, on pages that have no **Small print**.

## Video

Play a Shopify-hosted video or a YouTube or Vimeo link in a 16:9 frame with a cover image. The player only loads when a shopper presses play, which keeps the page fast. Add a **Caption** and a **Caption detail**, such as the running time, under the video.

**Play button** places either a labelled button at the bottom left, showing your **Play button label (screen readers)**, or a round button in the center.

Under **Chapters**, enter up to three **Chapter labels** separated by commas to draw a ruler along the bottom of the video. Pick a **Chapter colors from product** to color it in that product's color; without one, the color scheme's fit marker and text colors are used. Leave **Chapter labels** blank to hide the ruler.
