## Featured product

Sell one product anywhere: image, price, variant picker, buy button, Shop Pay Installments and accelerated checkout. Images, videos and 3D models all play here. Add **Custom liquid** or app blocks under the form.

Choose a **Layout**:

- **Stacked** — the product's colorway band as a rail on the left, a large title, the fit line and buy button, and one image. It's the layout for new Featured product sections.
- **Product hero — edge to edge, 50 / 50** — a full-width band with the image beside the buy box.
- **Contained, image beside the form** — the image beside the full product form, inside the page width.

The section shows the product's fit line, and shoppers can use it to pick a size. Dragging the marker along the line — or using the arrow keys — snaps to a size and selects it in the variant picker, so the price, image and **Add to cart** button update to that size. See [How the fit line works](/docs/fit-line/how-the-fit-line-works).

## Featured collection

A grid or a carousel of products from one collection. **Card style** switches between the **Image card** and a **Boxed** card that suits accessories. Choose **Desktop layout** and **Mobile layout** separately.

Turn on **Show fit tabs** to add a row of chips above the products — *All* plus the four fit zones. Choosing a zone shows only the products whose `custom.fit_position` falls in it, without reloading the page. See [How the fit line works](/docs/fit-line/how-the-fit-line-works).

## Collection tabs

Several collections behind tabs — *Slim*, *Relaxed*, *Oversized* — so shoppers can browse without leaving the page. Add one **Tab** block per collection. Choose a **Layout**:

- **Copy and chips beside the products** — the heading, text and tab chips on the left, the chosen collection's products on the right.
- **Heading with tabs, products below** — the heading and tabs above, up to four products per row below.

Each tab has a **Color**, used for the active tab's underline in the stacked layout and for a small tick in the chips layout. Leave it blank to use the tab collection's `custom.color` metafield, or else the color scheme's colors in turn, starting with the fit marker color.

## Collection list

Your chosen collections, one **Collection** block each. Without an image, a collection shows its first product's image. Choose a **Style**:

- **Bands** — each collection as a tall band in its color, as tall as its product count compared with the largest collection. Bands is the style for new Collection list sections.
- **Image with heading below** — an image with the title and product count under it, and the collection's fit range: the stretch of the fit line its products cover, from closest to loosest.
- **Tiles, heading over the image** — a tall tile with the title and product count over the image, after your **Tile link label**.
- **Circles** — round thumbnails with a label.

A band's color is the block's **Color**; when that's blank, it's the collection's `custom.color` metafield, or else the color scheme's colors in turn, starting with the fit marker color.

## Shoppable image

A lifestyle photo with hotspots. Each **Hotspot** block links a product to a point on the image; set its **Horizontal position** and **Vertical position** in percent.

**Product card** sets where a hotspot's product shows: **Beside the image**, **Pop over the dot**, or **In-this-look list, cards over image** — a list beside the image shows every hotspot product with its colorway and price, and an *Add all* button; choosing a hotspot opens its card over the image. **Hotspot marker** draws each hotspot as a short band in the **Colorway of the product** or as a **Dot**.

## Bundle builder

Shoppers pick a set number of products from a collection and add them to the cart in one step. Each product adds its first available variant, so single-variant products such as accessories work best. The **Bundle discount** setting is display-only: create a matching automatic discount in **Discounts** so the price actually changes at checkout.

Choose a **Style**: **Colorway bands** shows tall slots that fill with each pick's colorway band, beside the products to choose from, each with its band down the edge. **Cards** shows product cards beside a summary panel.

## Gift card purchase

Sell your gift card from any page, with amount buttons and the option to send it to a recipient by email on a chosen date. The amounts come from the variants of your **Gift card product**.

**Show colorway band** draws a band down the gift card's edge. Pick a **Colorway product** to use its colors; without one, the band uses the fit marker color.

## Blog posts

Your latest articles, or articles you pick, as cards.

Choose a **Layout**: **Lead article and list** shows the newest article large, with the next ones listed under a heavy rule. **Cards in a row** shows each article as a card, swiping on phones.
