## Country and language selectors

When your store sells in more than one country or language (**Settings > Markets** and **Settings > Languages**), turn on the selectors in the header's mobile navigation, the top bar or the footer. Shoppers pick a country to see prices in its currency, and a language to switch translations.

**Show currency codes** in **Theme settings > Currency format** adds codes such as *USD* or *EUR* to prices — useful when several currencies share a symbol.

## Translate or reword theme text

Text built into the theme — button labels, cart messages, form errors, the fit zone names — lives in your store's language files, not in sections. QCut ships with English theme text.

1. Go to **Online Store > Themes**, click **⋯** next to QCut and choose **Edit default theme content**.
2. Search for the text you want to change, for example *Size guide* or *Add to bag*.
3. Edit it and save. Your change survives theme updates.


For other languages, use **Settings > Languages** with the Shopify Translate & Adapt app.

<Callout type="tip" title="Renaming the fit zones">
To rename the zones in every language at once, use **Zone names** in **Theme settings > Fit line**. To translate them per language instead, leave **Zone names** empty and translate *Close*, *Regular*, *Relaxed* and *Oversized* in the theme content. If you use the fit filter, the filter values must match the zone names shoppers see.
</Callout>
