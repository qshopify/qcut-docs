These sections put the [fit line](/docs/fit-line/how-the-fit-line-works) at the center of a page. Add them from **Add section** in the theme editor, on any page. Every product they show needs a `custom.fit_position` metafield; products without one are left out. See [Fit data](/docs/fit-line/fit-data).

## Fit hero

A home page opener: a large image on one side and, on the other, the heading, a line of text, buttons and one product's fit line, sizes in stock and model line. Pick the product with **Product**. Its fit line is a size picker, so shoppers can drag it and the button opens the product in that size.

Add up to three **Measurement callout** blocks to pin measurements on the image, such as *Thigh 33 cm · relaxed*. Place each one with **Horizontal position** and **Vertical position**, in percent of the image. Set **Heading level** to **H1** when the fit hero is the first section on the page.

## Fit map

A whole collection on one fit line, with one row per product type, so shoppers see at a glance which trousers, shirts and jackets run close and which run loose. Each product is a dot at its fit position, with its name beside it. Names are spread over several lanes so neighbors don't overlap.

Choose a **Collection** and how many **Products** to map. With no collection picked, the section maps the current collection on collection pages, or the product's first collection on product pages. On a product page, the current product is drawn in the fit marker color.

Turn on **Show product images** to replace each dot with a small photo of the product. Hovering or focusing a photo shows a larger card with the zone and the price. On phones, the map scrolls sideways inside its box.

## Fit shop

Shop by fit, not by size. One large fit line runs across the page and the products below it re-sort as the shopper drags the marker, so the pieces that sit closest to that fit slide to the front. Shoppers can also tap a zone name or use the arrow keys. A line under the fit line says how many pieces sit in the current zone.

- **Collection** — the products to choose from. With no collection picked, the section uses the current collection on collection pages, or all products elsewhere.
- **Products to choose from** sets how many products are considered, and **Products shown** how many show at once.
- **Start at** sets where the marker starts, from 0 (close) to 100 (oversized). With **Start at the last product viewed** on, the section opens on the fit of the product the shopper last looked at instead, and says so.

Without JavaScript, the first products simply show in collection order. The **fit** collection template uses this section under its banner. See [Collection pages](/docs/collections-and-search/collection-pages).

## Fit compare

Two to four products on one shared fit line, then a card for each with its garment measurements in one size. Use it to explain the difference between your cuts, for example three trousers from slim to wide.

Add a **Product** block for each product, up to four. **Short name** replaces the product title over its marker, such as *Cut 01*. The first product's marker is drawn in the fit marker color and the others in the text color.

**Compare size** sets the size whose measurements appear on each card. It must match a size in the products' `custom.measurements`, such as *M*. A product without that size shows its card without measurements.

## Size finder

A short quiz that gives the shopper a size in your size run. They answer two questions:

- **Usual size elsewhere** — a size they wear in other shops.
- **How do you like it to sit?** — **Closer**, **As usual** or **Looser**, which moves the result one size down, keeps it, or moves it one size up.

They can also enter their height, which is saved for their own reference only. The result, such as *Your size here: M*, is saved in the shopper's browser. From then on, every product page marks that size's row in the measurement tables and shows *Your size: M* with them, and the fit line of every product that comes in that size is drawn for it. See [Who the line is drawn for](/docs/fit-line/how-the-fit-line-works#who-the-line-is-drawn-for).

List your sizes in **Size run**, smallest first, separated by commas, such as `XS,S,M,L,XL`. Use the same names as your products' **Size** option values. The **fit-guide** page template includes this section.
