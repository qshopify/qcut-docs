QCut is a Shopify theme for apparel brands that sell on fit. Every product sits on one **fit line**, from close to oversized, so shoppers can see how a piece sits before it arrives — and pick their size by dragging the line instead of guessing from a chart.

## What you get

- **The fit line everywhere** — on product cards, the product page, quick view, the cart, predictive search and the collection's fit line view, drawn from one product metafield. Wherever it appears, shoppers can drag it to pick the size whose fit lands where they want it.
- **Sizing tools** — a size guide drawer, garment and body measurement tables, measurements on product images, a size finder that remembers the shopper's size, and a fit filter drawn as zones on a ruler.
- **Fit sections** — fit hero, fit shop, fit map and compare fits, alongside heroes, storytelling and commerce sections such as a bundle builder and shoppable image.
- **A complete store out of the box** — home page, collection, product, cart, search, blog, article, contact, FAQ, fit guide and account pages, all built from sections you can rearrange.
- **Built-in Shopify features** — filtering, predictive search, pickup availability, subscriptions, gift cards, accelerated checkout, Shop Pay Installments, Follow on Shop and the customer account component.
- **Accessible by default** — keyboard navigation (the fit line works with the arrow keys), visible focus, skip link and WCAG AA color contrast on every preset color scheme.
