## Choose a preset

QCut comes in three presets. Each one is a full set of theme settings — colors, fonts, fit line style — plus its own home, product and collection templates, header and footer.

<img width="1656" height="348" src="/images/docs/presets.png" alt="The three presets side by side: QCut (Halden) with a stone header and an orange fit marker, Rivet with cream text on dark navy and a Menu button, and Drape with a plum top bar, a stacked header and a serif heading." />

| Preset | Made for | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **QCut** | Everyday menswear basics (demo brand Halden) | Near-black text on stone, signal orange fit marker, ruler with a pointer |
| **Rivet** | Raw denim and workwear | Cream text on dark indigo, copper accents, zones renamed *Skinny, Slim, Straight, Relaxed* |
| **Drape** | Womenswear | Plum text on blush, pill buttons, a plain line with a dot, and cards that show where a hem falls |

Pick the preset closest to your catalog — every setting can be changed afterward. See [Presets](/docs/getting-started/theme-settings#presets) for the full comparison.

## Add the theme to your store

1. Open the QCut listing in the Shopify Theme Store and choose a preset from the style picker.
2. Click **Try theme** to preview it with your own products, or **Add theme** after purchase.
3. QCut appears in your **Theme library** in **Online Store > Themes**. It is not live until you publish it.


To try a second preset, add QCut again from the Theme Store with the other style selected. Each copy has its own settings, so you can compare them side by side in the theme library.

## Customize before you publish

Click **Customize** next to QCut in your theme library. The theme editor opens with your real products, collections and menus, so you can see exactly how your store will look.

A good first pass:

1. **Logo and colors** — set your logo in the **Header** section, then open **Theme settings** (the paintbrush icon) for the favicon, color schemes and fonts. See [Theme settings](/docs/getting-started/theme-settings).
2. **Fit data** — add the `custom.fit_position` metafield to your products. Products without it show no fit line. See [Fit data](/docs/fit-line/fit-data).
3. **Navigation** — QCut's header uses your **Main menu** and the footer uses your **Footer menu** from **Online Store > Navigation**.
4. **Home page** — replace the sample sections' images and copy, and remove any section you don't need.
5. **Product page** — open a product in the editor and arrange the product information blocks. See [Product page](/docs/products/product-page).

## Publish

When you're happy, go back to **Online Store > Themes**, click **⋯** next to QCut and choose **Publish**. Your previous theme stays in the library, so you can switch back at any time.

<Callout type="warning" title="Duplicate before you edit code">
If you or a developer plan to edit theme code, always duplicate the theme first (**⋯ > Duplicate**) and make changes on the copy. See [Custom code](/docs/advanced/custom-code).

</Callout>

## Install from a file

If you received QCut as a `.zip` file, go to **Online Store > Themes**, click **Add theme > Upload zip file**, and choose the file. Don't unzip it first.
