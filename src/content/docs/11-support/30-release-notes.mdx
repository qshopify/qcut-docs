## 1.0.0

The first release of QCut — a theme for apparel brands that sell on fit. Every product sits on one fit line, from close to oversized, so shoppers can see how a piece sits before it arrives.

### The fit line

- One horizontal line, drawn from product metafields, on product cards, the product page, quick view, featured product, the cart drawer and cart page, product rows and predictive search.
- Two modes: fit (close to oversized) and length (where a hem falls), with an optional second marker for a compared cut or the fit after washing.
- The fit line is a size picker wherever it appears: product page, quick view, featured product, fit hero, product cards and the cart. It's drawn for one wearer — the shopper (from one body measurement, or their saved size) or else the model — and dragging it, or using the arrow keys, picks the size whose fit lands there. On cards, quick view opens on that size; in the cart, the line item's size changes in place.
- *Looser* or *closer than last viewed*: every product card and row marks the fit of the product the shopper last looked at.
- Size guide drawer: measurement tables open from a **Size guide** button next to the Size option.
- Ruler or plain line, pointer or dot marker, and renameable zones in Theme settings.
- Measurement panel on the product page: garment and body tables in one switch, the selected size highlighted, model info and a fit tip.
- **Size finder** section: two answers, and the shopper's size is marked on every product page.
- **Fit shop** section: one big fit line the shopper drags from close to oversized, and the pieces that sit nearest slide to the front. It opens on the fit of the product they last looked at.
- **Fit map** section: a whole collection on one line, one row per product type.
- **Fit hero** with image callouts, and a **Fit compare** section for up to four cuts on one shared line.
- Collection fit line view, and a fit filter drawn as zones on a ruler.
- Cart drawer free shipping progress drawn as a ruler.

### Shopping

- Product page built from individual blocks: title, price, rating, variant picker with swatches, fit, buy buttons, inventory, pickup availability, delivery estimate, trust badges, collapsible tabs, share, custom liquid and app blocks.
- Accelerated checkout and Shop Pay Installments on the product page, featured product and quick view. Subscriptions, pre-orders and gift cards with recipient forms.
- Images, Shopify-hosted video, YouTube, Vimeo and 3D models with AR in the product gallery.
- Quick view and quick add from product cards, with sizes in stock on cards.
- Cart drawer, cart notification or cart page, with order notes, discounts, unit prices and a free shipping goal.
- Filtering, sorting, pagination and predictive search. Related and complementary recommendations, and recently viewed products.
- Alternate templates, including Product · editorial (stacked images beside a sticky buy box) and Collection · fit (a cover banner, a fit shop and the fit line view).
- The product gallery stays beside the details on desktop, with the main image sized to the screen.

### Content

- Heroes: fit hero, image banner, split, slideshow, video, statement and page header.
- Storytelling: rich text, image with text, multicolumn, collage, testimonials, logo list, FAQ, comparison, timeline, countdown, video and custom liquid.
- Commerce: featured product, featured collection (with tabs and a carousel), collection list, shoppable image, bundle builder and gift card purchase.
- Blog in grid, list and sidebar layouts.

### Store-wide

- Three presets — QCut (everyday basics), Rivet (denim and workwear) and Drape (womenswear) — each with its own templates, header and footer.
- Four header layouts, mega menu or dropdown navigation with shop by fit, and sticky, compact and transparent options. Announcement bar and top bar.
- Four footer layouts, including Fit line: the four fit zones as large links along a ruler and the wordmark across the page. Signup, footer menus, social media icons, Follow on Shop, payment icons, and country and language selectors.
- Shopify's customer account component in the header on desktop and mobile.
- Newsletter popup, cookie banner (with Shopify's Customer Privacy API) and info drawer.
- Five color schemes with fit line colors, and font pickers for headings, body text and measurements.
- Keyboard navigation, visible focus, skip link and WCAG AA contrast.
- [JavaScript events](/docs/advanced/developer-events) for the cart, variant selection, drawers, search, newsletter signups and the size finder.
