## Blog layouts

Pick a **Layout** in the **Blog posts** section:

- **Grid** — cards with an optional featured latest article. The default blog template uses it.
- **Index list (grouped by month)** — a compact archive for frequent publishers.
- **Grid with sidebar** — a blog search, tags and a newsletter signup beside the grid.
- **Rows** — one ruled row per article: image, tag and reading time, title, and the product the article is about, marked with a tick in that product's color.

QCut also includes two alternate blog templates you can assign to a blog: **list**, set to the index list layout, and **sidebar**, set to the grid with sidebar.

Cards use each article's image, title and excerpt. Write a short excerpt in the article editor; without one, the start of the article is used. Tag filters use Shopify's own tag pages.

To link an article to a product, fill the article's `custom.product` metafield with a product or a list of products. See [Product data](/docs/products/product-data).

## Articles

Articles show the title, publish date, author and featured image, then tags and share links. **Show "In this story" contents** builds a table of contents from the article's H2 subheadings. Comments appear when you allow them on the blog, and are paginated.

- **Show colorway rail** runs a band of colors beside the reading column — the colors of the first product in the article's `custom.product` metafield, or else colors from the section's color scheme.
- **Show products in this article** adds an *In this article* list of the products in `custom.product`. Without the metafield, the list is hidden.

The default article template ends with a **Featured blog** section showing more articles.
